# Adoption metrics (simple leadership view)

Track monthly. Prefer a few honest numbers over a vanity dashboard.

| Metric | Definition | Source ideas |
|--------|------------|--------------|
| Active makers | Distinct users who edited/ran a flow or app in 30 days | Admin / CoE reports |
| Copilot scenarios in use | # of approved scenarios with tip sheets | Value Hub tracker |
| Intake volume | New use cases submitted | Intake list |
| Median time-to-triage | Days from Submitted → Triaged | Intake list dates |
| Deployed patterns | Solutions marked Deployed | Intake hub |
| Qualitative wins | Hours saved / error reduction stories | Sponsor quotes |

## Narrative template for leadership

> This month we triaged **N** ideas, deployed **M** patterns, and ran **K** enablement sessions. Top win: **[story]**. Top risk: **[governance or capacity]**. Next focus: **[persona or process]**.
